About Elvin Kakomo

Independent developer, building mobile games and apps inspired by Zimbabwe and Africa.

I’m Elvin Kakomo, an independent developer. I make mobile games and apps inspired by everyday places, streets and stories from Zimbabwe and across Africa.

This site is my developer studio page — the public home for the games and apps I publish, including privacy policies, terms, and support information. It is also the official developer website used on Google Play.

I work with Startum Africa. For questions about the apps, email appsupport@startumafrica.com.

The first title here is Cops & Robbers, a Zimbabwe-inspired 3D endless runner for Android, coming soon to Google Play.
