Cops & Robbers

Support & FAQ

Short answers about the game, ads, and your save data.

How do I contact you?

Email appsupport@startumafrica.com. Please mention that your message is about Cops & Robbers, and include your device model and Android version if you are reporting a problem.

Does the game have ads?

Yes. Cops & Robbers is free to play and is supported by ads. Watching an ad is always optional and can give you a reward, such as a free bribe, double coins, or free diamonds in the shop. An occasional full-screen ad may also appear between runs. There are no banner ads, no in-app purchases, and no “remove ads” product.

I uninstalled the game or lost my progress. Can you restore it?

Progress is saved only on your device — coins, diamonds, inventory, high scores and settings such as audio toggles. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or switching to a new device will reset that progress. There is no cloud save, so we cannot restore it from our side.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play without creating an account or signing in. The game does not ask for your name, email address or password.

Where can I download the game?

Cops & Robbers is coming soon to Google Play. This page will be updated with a working store link when the listing is live.

Where can I read the privacy policy and terms?

See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
