Privacy Policy for Cops & Robbers

Effective date: 12 November 2026. Developer: Elvin Kakomo.

This policy describes how the Android game Cops & Robbers (the “App”) handles information. It is written to match how the App actually works. If something is not listed here, we do not do it.

No accounts

The App does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide a name, email address, or password to play.

Local save only

Your progress (coins, diamonds, inventory, high scores, settings such as audio toggles, and similar game data) is stored only on your device. The developer does not operate a game server that receives this save data.

Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent (UMP)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads (for example a free bribe, double coins, or shop diamonds) and interstitial ads between runs may appear, according to the rules in the game. There are no banner ads and no in-app purchases.

On launch, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). You can manage consent through the form presented by UMP when it is required.

To provide, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID (and related advertising identifiers)
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data (for example impressions, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed)

This information is shared with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, as described in Google’s policies. The developer does not sell your data.

Google’s explanation of how partners use data is here: https://policies.google.com/privacy/partners.

Google’s AdMob / advertising privacy information is here: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and it is not designed as a children’s app under COPPA or similar laws. We do not set AdMob as child-directed, and the maximum ad content rating requested is T (Teen).

In-app purchases

The App has no in-app purchases and no “remove ads” product. Monetization is advertising only.

What the developer does not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run our own analytics, crash, or marketing service that collects personal information from the App, other than what Google processes for ads as described above.
  • We do not require you to provide contact details to play.

Changes

If the App’s data practices change, this policy will be updated at this URL.
