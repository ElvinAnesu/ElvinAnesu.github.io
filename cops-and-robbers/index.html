Grab the loot and run

Dodge the cops through the streets and bush of Zimbabwe.

Run, dodge and bribe your way to freedom in Cops & Robbers, an endless runner set in Zimbabwe. Play as Kax or Anne, two robbers with a bag full of loot and three determined cops right behind them. Swipe between three lanes, jump, slide and keep running for as long as you can.

Features

  • Simple swipe controlsMove between three lanes, jump and slide with one-finger controls.
  • Two playable robbersRun as Kax or Anne, and try to stay ahead of three chasing cops.
  • Coins and diamondsCollect coins as you run, and rarer diamonds. In the shop you can swap coins for diamonds, or diamonds for coins.
  • Shields and extra livesBuy shields and extra lives to survive longer. You can carry up to four extra lives in a run.
  • Bribe the copsBusted? You have five seconds to pay the cops in diamonds and keep running. Every bribe in a run costs double the last one.
  • Music and sound you controlTurn music and sound on or off from the game. Your choice is saved on the device.

Three zones

Mbilez

Busy streets, past places such as Soko, Matapi, Magaba, KwaMai Ju and Chillspot.

Tonaz

City streets with shops and signs such as OK Supermarket, Panmart, Speke, Fourth and Chicken In.

Bhundu

The open countryside — a change of pace from the city lanes.

Free to play

Cops & Robbers is free, and it has ads. Watching an ad is always optional and can give you a reward, such as a free bribe or double coins. There are no in-app purchases.
